About Velobank

Banking built for how Nigerians actually live.

We started Velobank because opening an account, sending money, and tracking spending shouldn't feel like paperwork. Three years in, we're still building around that one idea.

Our mission

Make everyday banking feel instant, fair, and a little bit delightful.

Lagos moves fast — your bank should too. Velobank was built by a small team of engineers and designers who got tired of banking apps that felt like an afterthought. We wanted transfers that land in seconds, savings that actually grow, and a support team that picks up the phone.

Today, Velobank is a licensed digital bank serving individuals and small businesses across Nigeria — but the goal hasn't changed: build the bank we'd want to use ourselves.

What we stand for

The principles behind every feature we build.

⚡

Speed by default

If something can happen instantly, it should. Transfers, account opening, card freezes — no spinners, no "please wait 24 hours."

🔍

No fine print

Fees, limits, and interest rates are shown upfront in plain language — not buried in a 40-page terms document.

🛡️

Security first

Every account is NDIC insured and protected with 256-bit encryption and biometric login. Your money's safety isn't a feature — it's the foundation.

🤝

Built with our users

Most of what's in the app came from feedback — real complaints, real requests, turned into features within weeks, not years.

🇳🇬

Proudly Nigerian

Designed in Lagos, for Lagos — and for everyone across Nigeria who deserves a bank that understands how they actually move money.

📈

Growth, not gimmicks

Savings goals with real, transparent interest. No lottery-style "spin to win" — just steady progress toward what you're saving for.
