This Privacy Policy explains how Velobank collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you visit our website or use your Velobank account.

1. Introduction

Velobank ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy applies to information collected through our website, dashboard, and any related services. By using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

2. Information we collect

Account and identity information

When you open an account, we collect your full name, date of birth, phone number, email address, home address, and identity verification details such as your NIN or BVN and a selfie photo.

Transaction data

We collect information about transfers, payments, bill payments, and balances — including the amount, date, time, and recipient or sender details — needed to provide and secure the Services.

Device and usage data

We may collect technical information such as your device type, IP address, browser type, and how you interact with our website and dashboard, to help us improve the Services and detect unusual activity.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Open, verify, and manage your account
  • Process transactions you authorize
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud and unauthorized activity
  • Comply with banking regulations and respond to lawful requests from regulators
  • Send you account notifications, statements, and important updates
  • Improve our products and develop new features

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your transaction data for advertising purposes.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only where necessary:

  • With regulators and law enforcement, where required by law
  • With partner banks and payment networks, to process transfers and card transactions
  • With service providers who help us operate the platform (such as identity verification or cloud hosting providers), under confidentiality agreements

We never share your password, transaction PIN, or full card CVV with any third party.

5. Data security

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using 256-bit encryption. Access to customer data within Velobank is limited to staff who need it to perform their roles, and all access is logged and monitored. We also offer biometric login and two-factor authentication to help protect your account.

6. Data retention

We retain account and transaction records for as long as your account is active, and for a further period afterwards as required by Nigerian banking and anti-money-laundering regulations — even if you close your account.

7. Your rights and choices

You can review and update most of your personal information from the Profile tab in Settings. You can also manage your notification preferences, and request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it where this doesn't conflict with our regulatory record-keeping obligations.

To make a request, contact us via the Contact page.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work correctly if cookies are disabled.

9. Children's privacy

The Services are intended for individuals aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll notify you through the app or by email before they take effect.

11. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests relating to your personal data, can be sent to our support team via the Contact page.
